Why This Program Exists

  

    
The Problem
  • 300 Managed Services issues/month tied to nightly analytics jobs
  • Alert fatigue & unclear ownership — failures ignored until client impact
  • Reactive work consuming planned capacity and eroding delivery confidence
  • Tableau Server: 500+ slow-loading workbooks degrading user experience

    
What We're Fixing
  • Noise vs. Signal — Alerts only fire when human action is required
  • Repeat Failures — Same root causes eliminated permanently
  • QA Gaps — Standards enforced at story level
  • Ownership Blur — Clear RACI: Managed Services / Core / Olympic
  • Optimization — Tableau remediation phased across 337 remaining workbooks

      03
      Leadership Escalation
    

    


      

        
Triggers — any one activates this phase

        

          

            
A
Client Severity Level

            
Issue affects a Tier 1 or enterprise client. Escalate immediately, do not wait for the L2 window to expire.

          

          

            
B
Scope of Impact

            
Three or more clients affected, or two or more data sources compromised in the same incident.

          

          

            
C
Recurring Issue Pattern

            
Same issue type at L1 or L2 more than once in a 30-day window — signals a systemic problem.

1

Week 1 Check-ins — Mon & Thu

              
Structured 20-min syncs: (1) resolution progress since last call, (2) active blockers and owners, (3) client-facing status update needed? Product Lead joins if a product rule change is in scope.

Same agenda as Week 1. If issue remains open at the Day 14 Monday call, leads must decide: extend escalation with a new owner, escalate further up the chain, or formally document a delayed-resolution plan with client notification.

Platform Quality & Reliability Program
A 160-day structured initiative to eliminate root-cause failures, enforce quality standards, clarify ownership, and bring Tableau workbook performance within acceptable thresholds across all CBRE clients.

  

    
Program Leadership
  • John Hernandez — Program Lead, Monitoring & Alerting, Tableau Performance
  • Megan Lane — Story / QA Redesign
  • Chris Jack — Operating Model Alignment
  • Dan Hernandez — Recurring Issue Elimination

    
Program Timeline
  • Start: April 13, 2026
  • Phase 1 End: May 4, 2026
  • Phase 2 End: May 25, 2026
  • Phase 3 End: July 7, 2026
  • Final Target: September 30, 2026

    
Success Criteria
  • Managed Services issues reduced to ≤50/month
  • 90% of Olympic JIRA stories meet QA standards
  • 90% reduction in highest-volume recurring failures
  • All priority Tableau workbooks under 8-second load time

    
Guiding Principles
  • Alerts only fire when human action is required
  • Every failure has a named owner and an SLA
  • QA is embedded in delivery, not bolted on after
  • Permanent fixes, not repeated incident response

  

  
"We don't win by responding faster to the same problems — we win by making them impossible to repeat."— PQRP Program Charter
